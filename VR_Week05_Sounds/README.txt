VR Week 5 — Original classroom sample audio / 第5周课堂示例音频

All three sounds were synthesized for this lesson. Use them as fallback samples when your team has not prepared audio. / 三个声音均为本课合成的示例，可在尚未准备音频时使用。

Folder / 文件夹:
Assets/07_Sounds/BGM/soft_pad_loop_24s.wav — background music, 24 sec, Loop on. / 背景音乐，24秒，开启Loop。
Assets/07_Sounds/SFX/airflow_loop_8s.wav — ambient sound, 8 sec, Loop on. / 环境音，8秒，开启Loop。
Assets/07_Sounds/SFX/task_complete_09s.wav — completion sound, 0.9 sec, Loop off, Play On Awake off. / 完成音效，0.9秒，关闭Loop和Play On Awake。

WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono. / WAV，44.1kHz，16位，单声道。

Unzip and copy only the 07_Sounds folder into your existing project's Assets folder. / 解压后仅将07_Sounds文件夹复制到现有项目的Assets文件夹。

Basic exercise: airflow + completion chime. Background music is optional. Set Spatial Blend=1 for positional audio. Start at low volume and adjust while listening in Quest. / 基础练习使用环境音与完成音效；背景音乐可选。需要空间定位的音源设置Spatial Blend=1，从较低音量开始，并在Quest中聆听调整。
